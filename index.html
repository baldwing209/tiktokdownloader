Understanding Quality and File Size

Choose from the quality settings actually displayed for your result. A resolution label describes the dimensions of the video; it does not tell you everything about how sharp or clear the recording will look.

The source matters. Soft focus, camera movement, and details lost in the original recording do not disappear simply because a file has larger dimensions. Preview your saved copy before relying on it for a presentation or project.

File size also deserves a look when you have limited storage. Video length, audio, and encoding all affect how much space a file uses. Avoid keeping several versions of the same clip unless you have a reason to do so.

Work Out Where a Download Stopped

When something goes wrong, identify which part of the process failed. Repeating the same submission without checking the result can leave you with duplicate attempts and no clearer answer.

Before the link is accepted

Check the address itself. Remove extra text and make sure it starts with a complete web address. Open it separately to see whether it leads to the intended TikTok post.

After the link is accepted

Read the page message. Passing a URL check only means the address matches the expected structure. It does not confirm that the video has been retrieved or that a downloadable version exists.

While the file is transferring

Look in your browser’s downloads panel for progress or an error. Check your connection and available storage if the transfer stops. Retry after addressing the issue shown.

After the transfer finishes

Locate the completed item through download history. If it does not play, confirm that the transfer finished and that your chosen media player supports the downloaded format.
