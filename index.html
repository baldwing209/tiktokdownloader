How to Start With a TikTok Video Link

  1. Open the individual video. Find the exact clip you want, rather than stopping at the creator’s profile or a page of search results.
  2. Copy its link. Use the video’s sharing options or copy the address of the individual post from your browser.
  3. Paste the complete address. Enter the URL in the box above. Leave out captions, hashtags, and any text that came with the link.
  4. Select Download. Read the message shown below the field. If download options are available, choose the file you want.

Keep the original video open while you work. That way, you can check the post or copy its address again without searching through your feed.

Why a TikTok Link Might Not Work

The pasted address is incomplete

Clear the field and copy the link again. A missing section, extra punctuation, or copied message text can prevent an address from being recognized. Try opening the URL in another tab to check it.

The link opens the wrong page

A profile or search page does not identify a single video. Return to the clip, open the individual post, and copy its own address before trying again.

The original video is unavailable

Check whether you can still open the source post. If the video is no longer accessible there, repeatedly submitting its link is unlikely to produce a different result.

No file appears after submission

Read the message beneath the input field. Recognizing a link and retrieving a video are separate steps. An accepted address does not guarantee that a downloadable file is available.

A download begins but does not finish

Check your connection and look at the download status in your browser. Before starting again, confirm whether the first attempt failed or is still in progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I enter in the download field?

Enter the address of one TikTok video. Avoid pasting a creator’s profile, a search page, or a full message containing several links.

Do I need to install an app to use this page?

No separate application is needed to open this page and submit a link. The input works through your web browser.

Can every TikTok video be downloaded?

No. A downloadable result depends on the video being accessible and supported by the connected service.

Will every video have the same quality options?

Do not assume that every post offers identical resolutions or formats. Check the options returned for the specific link you submit.

Where does a downloaded file go?

That depends on your browser settings. Look in the browser’s downloads list to identify the file and locate it on your device.

Why does a valid link not produce a download?

A valid address only confirms that the link has an accepted structure. Retrieving the video also requires an available source and a working download service.

Can I share a saved video on another platform?

Ask the creator before reposting their work, keep their credit attached, and follow any conditions they provide.
